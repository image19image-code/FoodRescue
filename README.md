# FoodRescue

## Mission

FoodRescue is a smart food rescue platform designed to reduce food waste by analyzing, prioritizing, and matching surplus food with suitable recipient organizations and communities.

## Core Problem

Large amounts of edible food can become waste because surplus is discovered too late, rescue opportunities are difficult to prioritize, or suitable recipients cannot be identified quickly enough.

## Core Solution

FoodRescue creates an intelligent rescue workflow that connects surplus food with potential recipients and tracks the rescue process from detection to measurable impact:

**Surplus Food**  
↓  
**Analysis**  
↓  
**Rescue Priority**  
↓  
**Smart Matching**  
↓  
**Rescue Operation**  
↓  
**Impact Tracking**

## Initial MVP

The first version includes:

- Surplus food submission
- Food rescue priority scoring
- Smart recipient matching
- Rescue operation status tracking
- Impact dashboard
- Location-based rescue intelligence
- Environmental condition monitoring
- Rescue history
- Multilingual interface architecture

## Intelligence Features

FoodRescue combines several decision layers to support rescue operations:

- **Priority Analysis** — evaluates urgency using food type, quantity, availability, and estimated expiry.
- **Smart Matching** — identifies suitable recipient organizations based on available surplus and matching criteria.
- **Environmental Intelligence** — uses location-based weather information to assess potential operational conditions.
- **Impact Tracking** — estimates rescued food, meals, CO₂ avoided, and other impact metrics.
- **Rescue Command Center** — tracks active rescue operations and completed rescues.
- **Forecasting Layer** — uses rescue history to support future surplus forecasting.

## Long-Term Vision

Build a global food-rescue intelligence platform capable of identifying recurring food surplus before it becomes waste, prioritizing time-sensitive rescue opportunities, and coordinating rescue operations between businesses, organizations, and communities.

The long-term vision is to create a connected and measurable food-rescue network where surplus food can move rapidly from potential waste to meaningful social and environmental impact.
